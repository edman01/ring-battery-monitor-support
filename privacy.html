Privacy policy

Ring Battery Monitor · Updated 26 September 2026

Ring Battery Monitor’s data controller is Eetu Mänttäri, Finland. Contact: edman.build@gmail.com.

Data the app uses

With your Oura authorization, the app reads battery levels, timestamps, charging status and ring details, including model, size, setup information and firmware. It uses these to display history and calculate estimates on your iPhone. It does not request your Oura email, sleep, activity, heart-rate or location data. There are no advertising or analytics SDKs, and we do not sell your data.

Storage and sign-in

Battery data travels directly from Oura to your device. Saved history and ring details stay on the device until you disconnect or delete the app; they are file-protected and excluded from backups. Sign-in credentials are protected in the device-only iOS Keychain. Up to 20 connection diagnostic entries are kept locally, without credentials or battery readings.

You sign in on Oura’s website; we never receive your password. Cloudflare hosts our sign-in service. It exchanges and renews access credentials but does not receive battery readings. Temporary sign-in records expire within ten minutes; used records are deleted and expired records are normally removed within a further 15 minutes. Credential handoffs are encrypted. Deleted records may remain in Cloudflare recovery backups for up to 30 days.

Short-lived, hashed IP addresses help prevent abuse. Our service does not keep request logs, but Cloudflare processes network metadata to operate and secure its infrastructure.

Purchases, exports and support

Apple handles payments. The app checks verified purchase information on your device; we do not receive card details or send purchase transactions to the sign-in service. CSV and PDF exports are created locally and shared only when you choose. Temporary exports older than 24 hours are cleared when a new export is prepared. Copies you save or share elsewhere remain there.

If you email support, Google processes your email and any attachments. We keep the correspondence only as long as needed to handle your request and any related dispute.

Your choices and rights

Demo mode needs no Oura connection. To clear saved history, credentials, ring details and generated exports, open Account → Oura account → Disconnect. To revoke future access, open the Oura app → Settings → App Integrations → Ring Battery Monitor and revoke access. Neither action cancels an Apple subscription or deletes your Oura account.

We process connected data to provide the service under our contract with you. Security and support rely on our legitimate interests in protecting the service and answering requests. You control Oura authorization and can revoke it at any time. Contact us to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, or to object to processing. You can also complain to a data protection authority, including Finland’s Data Protection Ombudsman.

Service providers and this website

Oura supplies ring data, Cloudflare hosts sign-in, Apple handles purchases, Google handles support email, and GitHub hosts these pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. We add no tracking scripts or cookies to this website. Providers may process data outside the EEA under the safeguards described in their policies: Oura, Cloudflare, Apple, Google and GitHub.
