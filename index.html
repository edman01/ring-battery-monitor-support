Ring Battery Monitor

An independent iPhone app for Oura battery history, charging records and runtime estimates.

Support

Email edman.build@gmail.com. Include your app version, iOS version and a short description of the problem. Never send passwords or access tokens.

For missing readings, sync your ring in the Oura app, then refresh Ring Battery Monitor. Estimates depend on available readings and your usage; they do not measure battery health.

Developed by Eetu Mänttäri in Finland. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Oura.
