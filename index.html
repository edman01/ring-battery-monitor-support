Ring Battery Monitor

An independent iPhone app for Oura battery history, charging records and runtime estimates.

TestFlight beta

Requires iOS 26 or later. All beta features are available without an app subscription, purchase or trial countdown. Explore the labelled demo without signing in, or connect Oura to use your own ring’s data.

Oura Ring Gen3 and later require an active Oura Membership to share data with this app through the Oura API. Oura Membership is provided by Oura and is not included in beta access. Gen2 does not require Oura Membership, but we have not verified that Gen2 supplies the battery data needed by this app. See Oura’s API requirements.

Support

Email edman.build@gmail.com. Include your app version, iOS version and a short description of the problem. Never send passwords or access tokens.

For missing readings, sync your ring in the Oura app, then refresh Ring Battery Monitor. Estimates depend on available readings and your usage; they do not measure battery health.
